[cllScrollToStepTop] enhances Clarity steppers (clrStepper) by automatically scrolling the viewport to the newly active step panel upon transitions. In addition, when the user clicks the "Next" button on an invalid step, the directive smoothly scrolls directly to the first validation error (clr-control-error) and focuses the invalid control.

Interactive Stepper Demo

Try clicking Next without filling required fields to see auto-scrolling to the first validation error, or complete the fields to experience smooth auto-scrolling to the next active step header.
