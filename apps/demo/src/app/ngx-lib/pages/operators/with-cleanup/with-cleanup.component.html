withCleanup manages the lifecycle of resources across complex update pipelines. When the projected operation succeeds, all superseded resources (identified by replacedOldName) are deleted. If the projected operation fails, any newly created resources (isNew: true) are rolled back and deleted, preventing partial state leaks.
