[ngxScrollIntoView] smoothly scrolls the target element into the user's viewport upon creation or when its bound boolean expression evaluates to true. It is ideal for error banners, confirmation dialogs, or dynamically expanded sections.

Directive Properties

Inputs

  • ngxScrollIntoView: boolean | '' — Whether to scroll. Defaults to true.
  • scrollOptions: ScrollIntoViewOptions — Options passed to scrollIntoView().
